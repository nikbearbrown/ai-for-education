# University textbook catalog

[Browse the subject catalog](textbooks.md) or choose the expanded indexes: [standalone books](books.md) and [books and course materials](books-and-courses.md). Start with one edition, confirm it fits your students, and check the rights for both its text and assets before adapting or exporting any part of it. A public reading link does not grant redistribution rights.

This catalog is the starting point for the [Medhavy course-building workflow](../docs/). Check the [INFO 6205 pilot](../docs/info-6205-pilot.md) to see what a local textbook scan and basic cartridge test can verify.
